Crypto futures, 5 minute meinCrypto futures in 5 minutes

Affiliate ke 80% sawaal in 6 shabdon se aate hain: spot, futures, long/short, margin, leverage, notional. Yeh samajh gaye toh baaki sab aasaan hai.

About 80% of affiliate questions come back to six words: spot, futures, long/short, margin, leverage, notional. Once these click, everything else is easy.

Spot vs FuturesSpot vs Futures

Spot = asli coin khareedna, jaise dukaan se sona khareedna. Futures = coin ke price pe ek contract. Coin haath mein nahi aata, sirf price ke upar-neeche hone ka profit ya loss INR mein settle hota hai. Niyam pe sab kuch futures/options hai, INR mein settle hota hai.

Spot means buying the actual coin, like buying gold from a shop. Futures are a contract on the coin's price. You never hold the coin; only the profit or loss from the price move settles, in INR. Everything on Niyam is futures or options, settled in INR.

Long & Short

Long = "price badhega" wala trade. Short = "price girega" wala trade. Futures mein dono equally aasaan hain, isliye girte market mein bhi trading hoti hai.

Long is a bet that the price will rise. Short is a bet that it will fall. In futures both are equally easy, which is why people trade in falling markets too.

Margin & LeverageMargin & Leverage

Jaise ₹10 lakh ka flat ₹1 lakh down payment pe. Down payment = margin. 10 lakh ÷ 1 lakh = 10x leverage. Niyam pe max . Leverage profit aur loss dono ko badhata hai.

Like buying a ₹10 lakh flat with a ₹1 lakh down payment. The down payment is the margin; 10 lakh ÷ 1 lakh = 10x leverage. Niyam allows up to . Leverage magnifies both profit and loss.

Notional

Position ka poora size. ₹10,000 margin × 10x = ₹1,00,000 notional. Saari fees notional pe lagti hain, isliye yeh shabd sabse important hai.

The full size of the position. ₹10,000 margin × 10x = ₹1,00,000 notional. Every fee is charged on notional, which makes this the most important word here.

Perpetual

Aisa futures jiski koi expiry nahi. Jab tak chaho hold karo. Price ko spot ke paas rakhne ke liye har 4 ya 8 ghante funding hoti hai.

A future with no expiry date; hold it as long as you like. Funding every 4 or 8 hours keeps its price close to spot.

INR market vs USDT marketINR market vs USDT market

INR market (BTC-INR) mein price rupees mein, USDT market (BTC-USDT) mein dollar-stablecoin mein dikhta hai. Fees dono mein same hain. Deposit/withdraw hamesha INR mein.

The INR market (BTC-INR) quotes prices in rupees; the USDT market (BTC-USDT) quotes in a dollar stablecoin. Fees are the same in both. Deposits and withdrawals are always in INR.

04 · Options

Options feesOptions fees

Options ki fees futures se bhi kam hain, aur ek cap hai taaki sasta option khareedne wale ki fee premium kha na jaaye.

Options fees are even lower than futures, and there's a cap so fees never eat a cheap option's premium.

Call

"Price upar jaayega" ka right. Jaise ghar ka token amount — fixed price pe khareedne ka haq.

The right to gain if the price rises, like a token amount that locks a purchase price.

Put

"Price neeche jaayega" ka right. Jaise insurance — girne pe kaam aata hai.

The right to gain if the price falls, like insurance that pays out on a drop.

Premium

Option ki keemat, jo buyer seller ko deta hai. Buyer ka max loss = premium.

The option's price, paid by the buyer to the seller. A buyer's maximum loss is the premium.

Strike · Expiry

Strike = tay kiya hua price. Expiry = option kab khatam hoga. Daily expiry bhi hote hain.

Strike is the agreed price. Expiry is when the option ends. Daily expiries exist too.

ITM / OTM

ITM (In-The-Money) = expiry pe option ki value hai. OTM = value zero.

ITM (in the money) means the option has value at expiry. OTM means it's worth zero.

Buyer vs SellerBuyer vs Seller

Buyer premium deta hai, kabhi liquidate nahi hota. Seller premium leta hai, margin rakhta hai, liquidate ho sakta hai.

The buyer pays the premium and is never liquidated. The seller collects it, posts margin and can be liquidated.

Notional

Underlying price × quantity. Trading aur delivery fee isi pe calculate hoti hai.

Underlying price × quantity. Trading and delivery fees are calculated on this.

Cap

Fee ki upper limit. Fee kabhi premium ke se zyada nahi hogi.

The fee ceiling. The fee can never exceed of the premium.

05 · Perpetuals

Funding

Funding fee nahi hai. Yeh longs aur shorts ke beech ka chhota payment hai. Niyam isme se ek paisa nahi kamata.

Funding is not a fee. It's a small payment between longs and shorts. Niyam earns nothing from it.

∞

Perpetual ki expiry nahi hoti, toh uska price spot price se door bhaag sakta hai. Funding ek rassi ki tarah hai jo dono ko baandh ke rakhti hai. Jis side zyada bheed hai (jaise sab long hain aur price spot se upar chala gaya), wo side doosri side ko thoda sa "kiraya" deti hai. Isse balance wapas aata hai.

A perpetual never expires, so its price could drift away from spot. Funding is the rope that keeps the two tied together. The crowded side (say everyone is long and the price runs above spot) pays a small "rent" to the other side, which pulls things back into balance.
